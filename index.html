Before you install

Open source

All of BLDR's code is public on GitHub under the MIT licence. Anyone can read it, check what it does with their data, or build their own version. The app itself costs nothing.

See the code on GitHub

Your own AI key

The Coach runs on Claude using an API key you create with Anthropic and paste into Settings. Anthropic bills you directly, roughly 5 to 15 cents for each workout the Coach writes. There's no subscription, and BLDR doesn't take a cut.

It runs the timer for you

Press start and BLDR times each exercise and says the name of the next one out loud. It keeps going with your phone locked, and Pause and Skip are on the lock screen.

Your workouts stay on your phone

You don't need an account. Workouts and history are saved on your phone only. If you use the Coach, it sends Anthropic what you type, the length and equipment you pick, and the names of your exercises.

Get BLDR on your phone

Download the app file and open it on an Android phone. It takes about a minute. Android shows a couple of warnings for apps that aren't from the Play Store, and the install page shows what to tap.
