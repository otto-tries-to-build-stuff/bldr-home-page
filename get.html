Get BLDR

BLDR isn't on the Play Store. You download the app file from GitHub and open it on your phone. Android asks you to confirm a couple of times, and the steps below show what to tap.

BLDR.apkVersion 0.2 · Android 8.0 or later
Download BLDR.apk

Open this page on your Android phone and tap the button there. Older versions

Install it in four steps

  1. Download the file

    Tap Download BLDR.apk on your phone. If your browser asks whether to keep the file, tap Download.

  2. Open it

    Tap the download when it finishes, or find BLDR.apk in your Files app.

  3. Allow installs from your browser

    The first time, Android says your browser isn't allowed to install apps. Tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.

  4. Get past Play Protect

    Google Play Protect may say it doesn't recognise the app, because it isn't on the Play Store. Tap More details, then Install anyway.

Set up the Coach (optional)

The Coach uses your own Anthropic key, so drafts are billed to you, roughly 5 to 15 cents each.

  1. Sign in to the Claude Console and add some credit under Billing. This is separate from a Claude.ai subscription.
  2. Create a key under API keys.
  3. In BLDR, open Settings, paste the key and tap Save key.
