What's in the app, area by area.

AI Coach

Bring your own Claude key
  • Describe what you're training for in your own words, pick 15 to 60 minutes and tick the equipment you have.
  • It writes a full workout, with sets, timings and rests, in about 15 to 30 seconds.
  • It can add exercises that aren't in the library yet, marked as new.
  • Ask for changes in plain words, or have it start again.
  • Save it, then adjust anything in the builder.

Exercise library

  • Over 100 exercises across legs, back, chest, shoulders, arms, core, cardio and mobility.
  • Filter by type, equipment, movement pattern and secondary muscles.
  • Equipment from bodyweight and bands to barbells and machines.
  • Each one links to a demo video. By default that's a YouTube search, or you can pin your own link.

Workout builder

  • Set each exercise to timed, reps (sets × reps, with an optional weight) or manual, which ends when you tap.
  • Group exercises into sections that repeat for a number of rounds.
  • Add rests between exercises, between rounds and after a section, each timed or until you're ready.
  • Move exercises up and down, or remove them, as you build.

Run a workout

  • A "get ready" countdown before each exercise, with what's next.
  • A big timer ring, set checklists for reps, and an "up next" card.
  • Pause and skip from the screen or the lock screen.
  • The timer runs in the background, so it keeps going with the screen off.

Sound and voice

  • Cue tones for the last three seconds and for "go".
  • The phone's built-in voice names each exercise before it starts: "Next up: Skater Bounds".
  • "Rest" between exercises and "Workout complete" at the end.
  • All of it works with the phone locked.

Built-in help

  • A welcome tour the first time you open the app.
  • How-to guides in Settings, with animated walkthroughs.

Themes and accessibility

  • Light and dark themes, including the workout screen.
  • Works with large text sizes and Android's "remove animations" setting.

Your data

  • Workouts, exercises, history and settings live only on your phone.
  • You don't need an account to use it.
  • The Coach sends Anthropic what you type and your exercise names. Your workout history stays on the phone.
  • Your key is stored encrypted on the phone.
